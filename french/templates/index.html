How the translation is built

Designed around the Mannat Excel workflow.

01 · Read

Understands all seven columns

Serial number, timecodes, speakers, receivers, screenplay notes, and English dialogue stay associated.

02 · Translate

Maintains sentence continuity

Each 50-row batch receives surrounding dialogue and the uploaded character and story references.

03 · Deliver

Preserves the source workbook

A FRENCH TRANSLATION column is added while worksheet names, source cells, styling, and order remain intact.
